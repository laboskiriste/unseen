Unseen is on

Read your email as usual

Open Gmail in your browser. When an email hides a tracking pixel, Unseen blocks it and shows a small note above the email with the tracking company's name. Normal images load as before.

If Gmail was already open, reload it once so Unseen can start.

Open Gmail

When you want the images

Click Load images anyway in the note above an email to let that one email through. The switch in Unseen's toolbar menu turns blocking off everywhere.

Your privacy

Unseen runs only on mail.google.com and can't see any other site. It never reads, stores or sends your emails. It makes no network requests of its own: no servers, no analytics, no account. Blocking trackers stays free forever.
