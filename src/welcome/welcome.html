Unseen is on

Read your email as usual

Open Gmail in your browser. When an email hides a tracking pixel, Unseen blocks it and shows a small note above the email with the tracking company's name. Normal images load as before.

If Gmail was already open, reload it once so Unseen can start.

Open Gmail

When you want the images

Click Load images anyway in the note above an email to let that one email through. The switch in Unseen's toolbar menu turns blocking off everywhere.

Free, and Unseen Plus if you want it

Everything that protects you is free, and stays free: blocking trackers, the note in Gmail, and opening links in your emails without their click trackers, so the sender doesn't learn what you clicked. Nothing expires.

Unseen Plus adds your tracker history, month by month, with an export, for people who want to support Unseen. Pay what you want, from $5, once. Skip it and nothing changes.

Your privacy

Unseen runs only on mail.google.com and can't see any other site. It never reads, stores or sends your emails. No servers, no analytics, no account. It keeps a count of trackers blocked per day, on your device only. The only thing it ever sends is an Unseen Plus license key, if you buy one and enter it, to check it with the store.
