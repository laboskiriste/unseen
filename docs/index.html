Unseen

Stop email tracking in Gmail

Newsletters and sales emails hide invisible images that tell the sender when you open them. Unseen blocks those tracking pixels in Gmail on the web and leaves normal images alone.

  • Blocks 330+ known email trackers and hidden pixels
  • Runs only on Gmail, with no access to other websites
  • Nothing leaves your browser: no servers, no analytics, no account
  • Chrome, Edge and Firefox
