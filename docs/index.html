Unseen

Stop email tracking in Gmail

Newsletters and sales emails hide invisible images that tell the sender when you open them. Unseen blocks those tracking pixels in Gmail on the web and leaves normal images alone.

  • Blocks 330+ known email trackers and hidden pixels
  • Runs only on Gmail, with no access to other websites
  • No ads, no analytics, no account. Blocking happens entirely in your browser
  • Chrome, Edge and Firefox

Unseen Pro

Blocking is free and stays free. Unseen Pro adds new features on top, starting with version 0.2.0:

  • Clean tracking links: when you click a link in an email, Unseen opens the real address without the click tracker. The tracker is never contacted.

Every new user gets 30 days of Pro free. After that, Pro is $15 once: no subscription. If you don't buy it, only Pro turns off; blocking keeps working.

Payment, receipts, taxes and refunds are handled by Polar, the merchant of record. Your license key is checked with Polar about once a month; nothing else is sent. Questions and support: GitHub issues.
