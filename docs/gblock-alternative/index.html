Gblock is a well-made, actively developed Gmail extension. After a 30-day trial it costs $1.99 a month or $23.88 a year. If what you want is for senders to stop seeing when you open their emails, you don't have to pay a subscription for that.

When Gblock is the better choice

If you want a commercial product with support, and features beyond blocking, a subscription can be worth it. We'd rather you pick the right tool than ours.

When a free one is enough

If you mainly want pixels blocked, Unseen does that free, for good, shows which company tried to track each email, and its code is public. Trocker is free too and also cleans links, with access to all websites.
