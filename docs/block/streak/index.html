How to block Streak email tracking in Gmail

Checked November 14, 2026. Written by the developer of Unseen, a free tracker blocker for Gmail.

Streak is a CRM that runs inside Gmail. Senders who use it can turn on tracking for the emails they send.

What the sender sees

Through a tracking pixel, a tiny invisible image unique to you, the sender learns when and how often you viewed the email. Gmail loads images through its own proxy, which hides your IP address, but the open is still recorded. More on what Gmail does and doesn't hide.

Check whether an email has it

In Gmail, open the email, click ⋮ and then Show original, copy the whole text and paste it into the email tracker checker. It lists every tracking pixel it finds and runs entirely in your browser.

Block it

With Unseen (free)

Unseen recognizes 2 Streak tracking patterns, among 332 patterns from 184 companies, and also blocks tiny or hidden images. It blocks only the tracker, so the rest of the email looks normal, and shows a note naming who tried to track you. It can only see Gmail, and it's open source.

Without an extension

In Gmail's settings, set Images to Ask before displaying external images. That stops every tracking pixel, but also hides every normal picture until you click. Step-by-step guide.

Will the sender know I blocked it?

No. They see the same thing as when someone reads email with images off: no open is recorded.
