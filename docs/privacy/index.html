Privacy policy

Unseen: Email Tracker Blocker · Effective 31 October 2026

Unseen collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no servers, no analytics and no accounts.

What the extension can access

  • It runs only on https://mail.google.com. It has no access to any other website.
  • In the emails you open, it looks at the addresses of images to tell tracking pixels from normal images. It doesn't read, store or send the text of your emails, your contacts or your email address.

What it stores, and where

  • Your on/off setting.
  • A count of blocked trackers (the tracking company's name and the date) for the "last 7 days" view.
  • To avoid counting the same email twice, short one-way hashes of tracker addresses, never the addresses themselves.

All of this stays in your browser's extension storage on your own device. Entries older than 30 days are deleted automatically. Removing the extension deletes everything.

Sharing

The extension makes no network requests of its own, so no data is sent to us or to anyone else. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the source repository.
